# ADR-0000 Title, matching the file name

## Context
Single most important part. Include:
- trigger: what forced a decision
- requirements and constraints: Which part of the design docs apply. Link them.
- knowledge: knowns, uknowns and assumptions at the time of deciding
- forces in tension: the trade-off that made it a real decision (e.g. historical accuracy vs performance)
- source: relevant sources, including historical

## Decision
The final decision and rationale.

## Alternatives considered
Any alternative approach that was considered and a rationale why they were dropped. 
Keep it brief.

## Consequences
Consequences of the Decision, both positive and negative
